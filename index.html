Leadership & Creative Core

Core Team

We are always on the lookout for visionary talent. Meet the producers and creative executives steering the future of Side Hero Entertainment.

Faizuddin Siddiqui

Producer & Writer

Coming from a journalism background and being the younger brother of veteran actor Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Faiz started his industry journey as a writer with “Arthi” and “Adhura Khvaab”. Both stories were developed into full feature films produced under Side Hero Entertainment and screened at prestigious festivals in India and abroad. Through his NGO Lihaaz Foundation, he champions sugarcane farmers in Western UP.

Saba Siddiqui

Producer

Saba started her career as a schoolteacher in Dehradun and brings over 10 years of professional experience in education. Dedicated to real social issues, she co-founded the NGO Lihaaz Foundation. As a producer at Side Hero Entertainment, she is committed to producing meaningful cinema rich in human values and hard-hitting social truth.

Shiv Sharan Chowdhury

Associate Producer

Shiv brings ~25 years of seasoned expertise across Star News, Star TV, INX Media, Sony Sports, and leading film production houses. Currently engaged as Associate Producer at Side Hero Entertainment, his experience spans project execution, post-production, channel integration, and end-to-end studio operations.
